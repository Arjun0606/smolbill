how cloud is priced

Flat base + usage. Never a percent of your revenue.

A small flat base, then usage-based pricing metered on the engine's own operational usage — events ingested, invoices finalized, active billed customers — metered by smolbill itself. A customer doing $1M in revenue pays for their billing volume, not a slice of their money: cheaper than a 0.7% cut, while still scaling with growth. The Supabase model.
