Why smolbill

never a %

Flat-priced, not a cut of your money

Priced on events metered, never a percent of your billing volume. The thing Stripe Billing structurally can't drop, because it's their own revenue line.

provably correct

Your meter and invoice can't silently disagree

A reconciliation ledger recomputes the bill from your raw events on demand and shows you, line by line, exactly where anything drifted. Billing bugs that quietly overcharge people are the nightmare. This is the fix.

ai-native

Your agent sets the rules, never does the math

An MCP server lets Claude or Cursor configure billing by passing intent. There is no charge() tool. The deterministic engine does every cent, so a hallucinated decimal never touches money.

free dunning

Decline-aware failed-payment recovery

Retries failed payments on a configurable schedule, but stops hammering a dead card instead of tripping the bank's fraud limits. Lago and Chargebee paywall this. Here it's in the core.
