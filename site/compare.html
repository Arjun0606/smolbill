the honest part

Where they genuinely win.

tax

Merchant-of-Record tax handling

The MoR processors (and Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Dodo) calculate and remit global VAT/GST/sales tax for you. smolbill does not replace this — it routes collection to them. We compete on the billing, metering, and correctness layer, not on being your tax merchant.

maturity

Stripe Billing maturity

Stripe Billing is genuinely mature for plain subscriptions, with great dunning and high ingestion throughput. We win on usage-based correctness, neutrality, and being AI-native — not on being older.

scale

Raw scale

Metronome and Orb meter 100k+ events/sec on Kafka and ClickHouse. smolbill is a single binary on Postgres: we win on simplicity and provable correctness, not raw throughput (yet).

breadth

Lago breadth

Lago is a capable, full billing engine. Our edge vs Lago is no paywall on the basics, provable reconciliation, lighter ops, and a free MCP server.

the proof

Stripe paid ~$1B to buy Metronome rather than fix its own billing.

A payment processor's billing is a feature that taxes payment volume, not a product. The dedicated billing layer — Lago, Orb, Metronome — is worth billions precisely because billing is a separate, hard problem. Stripe, with the best billing of all of them, bought its way into usage-based billing instead of building it. That's the market thesis, validated by the incumbent.
